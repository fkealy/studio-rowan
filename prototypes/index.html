Website prototypes

Seven directions for studiorowan.com, all one-page, all built on the brand guidelines. Same copy, same palette, three different ways of turning the words into the picture.

  • 01Hemp

    Warm editorial. Type at scale on Hemp, Ivory and Olive blocks. The numbers are the imagery. GSAP only, very quiet motion.

  • 02Olive

    Deep colour, big statements. A three.js field of exactly 70,000 dots, one per pair of slippers a hotel throws away in a year, drifts behind the hero and disperses as you scroll.

  • 03Clay

    Structured and textural. A visible grid, the brand symbols used large, and a very slow three.js dune shader in Clay, Hemp and Ivory behind the wordmark.

  • 05Dots

    03's text layout without the grid lines, 01's "And again" stack, and the 70,000-dot hero on Olive.

  • 06Dots, new copy

    05 as the base with the revised website copy: the hotel slipper story, the Never-Ending Slipper with the poolside photograph, mission, and button CTAs throughout.

  • 08Bold

    Colour and size do the work. Full blocks of Apricot, Olive, Hemp, Onyx and Ivory that cross-fade as you scroll, one sentence filling the screen, the small print in narrow columns. Nothing else.

  • 07Room

    The same copy as a hotel room you arrive in: curtains draw open to morning light through the window, a door hanger asks you to leave less behind, the numbers sit on the room folio, the slipper is the turndown, and Work With Us is the room at night by lamplight.

Working assumptions

  • Display typeface. The guidelines specify Raptor V2 Premium, which is a licensed font we don't have yet. The prototypes use Outfit Light as a stand-in. It is set as a single CSS variable in shared/brand.css, so dropping Raptor in later is a one-line change. Inter is self-hosted as before.
  • Mission closing paragraph. Prototypes 01, 03 and 05 use the second version of the original closing paragraph; 06 and 07 use the revised copy supplied on 8 September 2026 ("we don't want sustainability to be the only improvement we make"). Prototype 02 uses the first, so both can be seen in context.
  • Buttons in 06 and 07. "Request samples", "Work with us" and "Explore the slipper" all open a pre-addressed email for now. "Explore the slipper" presumably wants a product page later, so its destination needs deciding.
  • Contact. All three use hi@studiorowan.com from the guidelines' letterhead. The current holding page uses info@studiorowan.co.uk, so this needs confirming.
  • Assets. The wordmark and the eight brand icons were extracted as vectors from the guidelines PDF into shared/brand/ and are injected by shared/brand.js.
  • Libraries. GSAP, ScrollTrigger and three.js load from cdnjs for the prototypes. The production build should vendor them under /vendor as before.
  • Motion. Every prototype honours prefers-reduced-motion, keeps content readable without JavaScript, and avoids anything that moves on its own beyond the slow hero backgrounds.
