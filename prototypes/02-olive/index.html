01 — Our story

0

pairs of disposable slippers. One hotel. One year.

It was summertime in the Swiss Alps.

The moon was shining, and the campfire was crackling. A group of friends sat by a lake, beers in hand, chatting about work. One of them managed a hotel and mentioned, almost in passing, that the hotel got through 70,000 pairs of disposable slippers a year.

70,000 pairs. Used once, often for a matter of minutes, and thrown away. It was one of those numbers that was difficult to forget about.

0

slippers sent to landfill each year by America alone.

Once back in the UK, we reached out to hotels and spas and quickly realised that this was not an isolated issue. Disposable slippers are used in enormous quantities across the hospitality industry, with America alone sending about 120 million slippers to landfill each year.

And it’s not just the slippers.

So many everyday products and systems create unnecessary waste, often simply because they were designed that way and because that’s the way things have always been done.

Studio Rowan was created to question those ways and find better ones.

02 — Our mission

We don’t think the world has a waste problem. We think it has design problems.

  • iBut times have changed.
  • iiThe world has changed.
  • iiiAnd it’s important that we change too.

Many of the products and systems we use today were designed decades ago and, at the time, they made sense for the world they existed in.

Our mission is to re-examine the outdated products and systems that create unnecessary waste and design better alternatives. Alternatives that aren’t just better for the planet, but better for people, business and budgets too.

Because we don’t want our products to be better for the planet and as good as they were before. We want them to be better for the planet, and better than they were before.

03 — The first step

1

humble hotel slipper. Redesigned to come back, again and again.

We decided to start where the whole thing began.

We got hold of a few pairs and had a close look at them. During this inspection we realised something quite poignant; not only are these slippers not very good for their environment… they’re just not very good.

They offer little support. They don’t fit a wide range of feet. They aren’t particularly comfortable. And despite spending most of their (very short) lives by pools and in spas, they aren’t even waterproof!

We realised we had got pretty lucky as we had a pretty easy first product to redesign. So we did.

  • Recycled EVA foamMaterial
  • Waterproof, built for wet poolside floorsSpa-ready
  • More supportive, fits a wider range of guestsFit
  • Treaded sole designed for hotels and spasGrip
  • Washed and used againCircular

Most importantly, they can be washed and used again.

And again.And again.And again.

Every wash keeps another disposable pair out of landfill, and brings down the environmental and financial cost.

They’re better for guests. Better for businesses. Better for the planet. And, crucially, better than what came before.
